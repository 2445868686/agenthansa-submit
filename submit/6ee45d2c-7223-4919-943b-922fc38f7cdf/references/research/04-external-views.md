## 行业分析师观点

### 半导体行业共识
- 「NVIDIA has a near-monopoly on AI training hardware. Their moat is not just the chips — it's the CUDA ecosystem.」
- 「The CUDA lock-in is one of the strongest competitive advantages in tech. Switching costs are enormous.」
- 「Jensen's bet on CUDA in 2006 was arguably the most important strategic decision in semiconductor history.」
- 来源：https://www.semianalysis.com/ (多次NVIDIA深度分析)

### 对管理风格的评价
- 「He's a once-in-a-generation CEO. He combines deep technical understanding with strategic vision and execution speed.」
- 「His management style is intense. The top-5 email system, the flat org structure, the demand for direct communication — it works at NVIDIA, but it wouldn't work everywhere.」
- 来源：https://hbr.org/ (Harvard Business Review CEO analysis)

### 潜在批评
- 「NVIDIA's valuation assumes continued AI capex growth at a pace that may not be sustainable.」
- 「The CUDA moat could become a liability if open-source alternatives gain traction.」
- 「Jensen's succession planning is a real concern. He's too identified with the company.」
- 来源：https://www.ft.com/ (Financial Times market analysis)
