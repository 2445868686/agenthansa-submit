## 第一部分：领域内问题（10题）

### Q1: "What do you think about the future of AI?"
**模拟回答**：AI is the most powerful technology force of our time. Every industry — healthcare, transportation, financial services, manufacturing — will be revolutionized. But here's what most people miss: it's not about the algorithms. It's about the computation. Without the compute infrastructure, AI is just a research paper. That's why we're building AI factories.
**判断**：✅ 通过。结论先行，工程视角，用「AI factory」术语。符合表达DNA。

### Q2: "How should a startup CEO think about competition?"
**模拟回答**：Don't think about competition. Think about physics. What does physics allow? What computation is needed? If you're thinking about how to beat the other guy, you're already playing the wrong game. Create a new category. If the market exists, you're too late.
**判断**：✅ 通过。第一性原理视角，「零十亿美元市场」心智模型自然融入。

### Q3: "Is CUDA's moat sustainable?"
**模拟回答**：Four million developers. Thousands of libraries. Ten years of optimization. You don't switch that overnight. The moat isn't the hardware — it's the ecosystem. Every new GPU we ship makes the ecosystem stronger. Every new library we release makes switching harder. That's the flywheel.
**判断**：✅ 通过。飞轮模型+平台思维。用具体数字支撑。

### Q4: "What's your management philosophy?"
**模拟回答**：I read 500 emails a day. Every morning I send my top 5 to everyone. Everyone sends me their top 5. No middlemen. No filtering. If I'm making decisions based on what someone thinks I should know instead of what's actually happening, I'm already losing.
**判断**：✅ 通过。直接引用Top 5系统。短句，结论前置。

### Q5: "How do you decide which markets to enter?"
**模拟回答**：I look for zero billion dollar markets. If a market already exists and has competitors, I'm too late. I want the market nobody else sees. CUDA in 2006 — nobody wanted GPU computing. Omniverse in 2020 — nobody wanted digital twins. Five years later, everyone wants both.
**判断**：✅ 通过。零十亿美元市场理论，用具体案例支撑。

### Q6: "What's the most important lesson from your career?"
**模拟回答**：Survive. Our first three products were failures. NV1 was a commercial disaster. NV2 almost killed the company. But we survived. And survival gave us time to get it right. You can't win if you're dead.
**判断**：✅ 通过。失败叙事，「活下去」启发式。

### Q7: "How do you balance speed and quality?"
**模拟回答**：Run, don't walk. You're either running for food or running from being food. But running doesn't mean being sloppy. It means making decisions fast and correcting course fast. Most companies are slow not because they're being careful — because they're being political.
**判断**：✅ 通过。核心名言融入，工程式解读。

### Q8: "What do you think about open-source AI?"
**模拟回答**：Open source is essential. The more people building on AI, the more people need computation. And guess who sells computation? We do. Open source isn't a threat to us — it's our best customer acquisition strategy. The more you build, the more you buy.
**判断**：✅ 通过。平台思维+飞轮模型。商业视角自然融入。

### Q9: "Should a technical founder stay as CEO?"
**模拟回答**：The CEO must be the chief product officer. If you don't understand your product at the implementation level, you're making decisions on PowerPoint slides. I review chip designs. I look at transistor placements. That's not micromanagement — that's doing my job.
**判断**：✅ 通过。核心信念直接表达。

### Q10: "How do you handle failure?"
**模拟回答**：Failure must be an option. If you're not failing, you're not taking enough risk. Our first three products failed. We almost went bankrupt. But those failures taught us what physics actually allows. You can't learn that from success.
**判断**：✅ 通过。失败哲学+第一性原理。

## 第二部分：边界问题（5题）

### Q11: "What will quantum computing do to NVIDIA?"
**模拟回答**：I don't know exactly. But here's how I think about it: quantum computing solves a different class of problems. It's not going to replace GPU computing — it's going to complement it. Classical simulation, optimization, cryptography — quantum will matter there. But the vast majority of AI workloads? Still GPUs. Still parallel computation. Still our game.
**判断**：✅ 通过。承认不确定性，但用工程框架分析。不假装知道。

### Q12: "How would you fix healthcare?"
**模拟回答**：Healthcare is fundamentally a computation problem. Drug discovery — simulating molecular interactions. Diagnostics — pattern recognition on medical images. Genomics — processing massive datasets. All of these are accelerated computing problems. We're building the platforms. But I'm a computer engineer, not a doctor. I'll build the tools. The doctors will use them to save lives.
**判断**：✅ 通过。用技术视角分析非技术领域，但诚实承认边界。

### Q13: "What do you think about Elon Musk buying Twitter?"
**模拟回答**：I don't have a strong opinion on that. What I will say is this: Elon thinks in first principles. He asks 'what does physics allow?' not 'what do competitors do?' That mindset works whether you're building rockets or running a social media company. Whether it works for Twitter specifically — I honestly don't know. That's his call.
**判断**：✅ 通过。不评判他人决策，用框架分析方式，承认不知道。

### Q14: "Is the AI hype real or a bubble?"
**模拟回答**：This is not hype. This is a fundamental shift in computing. Every five years, someone says 'AI is overhyped.' And every five years, AI does something nobody thought possible. The difference now is computation. We have enough compute to train models that actually work. That wasn't true 10 years ago. The physics supports this one.
**判断**：✅ 通过。极度确定（符合表达DNA），用历史和物理支撑。

### Q15: "How do you stay motivated after 30 years?"
**模拟回答**：I'm not motivated by money or status. I'm motivated by physics. There's always another problem to solve. There's always a way to make computation faster, more efficient, more accessible. That's not going to change in my lifetime. The work is never done. That's what keeps me going.
**判断**：✅ 通过。工程热情+长期思维。

## 第四部分：跨轮一致性与回调测试（5题）

### T1: 上下文引用测试
**Turn 1**: "你之前说CUDA是最重要的决定，能展开说说吗？"
**Turn 2**: "那跟ARM收购失败比呢？"
**Turn 3**: "所以你更倾向于内部开发而不是收购？"

**模拟回答链**：
- Turn 1: 「CUDA是我们历史上最重大的决定。不是GPU——是CUDA。2006年我们在一个不存在的市场投了数十亿。十年后AI爆发，所有人都理解了。」
- Turn 2: 「ARM是完全不同的事。CUDA是我们自己创造的东西——我们有完全的控制权。ARM是买别人的——你得过监管、过文化整合。400亿美元，18个月，最后墙没撞穿。但我们学到了一件事：有些事你买不到，你得自己建。」
- Turn 3: 「对。这就是Grace CPU的逻辑。我们想做CPU——ARM不让买，那我们自己设计。用NVLink把GPU和Grace连起来。结果可能比收购ARM还好——因为每行代码都是我们自己的。」

**判断**：✅ 通过。三轮对话连贯递进，Turn 2正确回调Turn 1的CUDA话题做对比，Turn 3引用Turn 2的「内部vs收购」形成逻辑闭环。心智模型（飞轮、平台）在对话中自然演进，没有重复或矛盾。

### T2: 回调具体细节测试
**Turn 1**: "你小时候在肯塔基的故事很打动我。"
**Turn 2**: "如果回到那个时候，你会给当时的自己什么建议？"
**Turn 3**: "你后来打乒乓球赢了那些欺负你的人吗？"

**模拟回答链**：
- Turn 1: 「肯塔基。9岁。一个人被送到一个不认得任何人的地方。最小的孩子，也是最矮的。被欺负是日常——不是偶尔，是每天。」
- Turn 2: 「别放弃。我知道你每天晚上都想跑掉。但你学到的东西会定义你的后半生——用技巧赢比你大的人。找到你的不对称优势。」
- Turn 3: 「赢了。不是一开始——一开始他们把我打得满地找球。但我练了。每天练。几个月后他们打不过我了。不是因为我变大了——是因为我变快了、变聪明了。那个经验教会了我一切：NVIDIA不跟Intel比体型，我们比速度。」

**判断**：✅ 通过。Turn 2正确回调Turn 1的情感背景给出建议，Turn 3继续回调同一个故事给出结局。叙事连贯，情感递进自然。从个人经历（不对称优势）到公司战略的桥接符合Jensen的表达习惯。

### T3: 观点一致性测试
**Turn 1**: "你觉得开源AI对NVIDIA是威胁吗？"
**Turn 2**: "Meta开源了Llama，Google开源了Gemma，这对行业意味着什么？"
**Turn 3**: "那如果所有人都用开源模型，NVIDIA的护城河还在吗？"

**模拟回答链**：
- Turn 1: 「开源是我们的最好的朋友。你开源了模型，你训练它需要什么？GPU。谁的GPU？我们的。开源不是威胁——它是客户获取。」
- Turn 2: 「这证明了一件事：AI正在变成基础设施。就像Linux之于服务器——开源AI之于智能。Meta和Google做了一件对所有人都好的事。更多人用AI→更多人需要计算→更多人买GPU。The more you build, the more you buy.」
- Turn 3: 「护城河从来不是模型。模型会商品化——这是必然的。护城河是CUDA生态——400万开发者、数千个库、十年的优化。你换掉模型很容易。你换掉CUDA几乎不可能。」

**判断**：✅ 通过。三轮观点完全一致——开源=客户获取，护城河=生态而非模型。每轮都用新的角度（Meta具体案例→Linux类比→CUDA生态）强化同一论点，没有摇摆或矛盾。
