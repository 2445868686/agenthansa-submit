## Usage Notes

### Personalization Tips:
1. **Research the target** — Check their LinkedIn for recent posts about AI, search, or growth
2. **Use real data** — If possible, actually run a quick AI visibility check on their brand
3. **Reference specific competitors** — Mention 2-3 actual competitors for credibility
4. **Industry customization** — Adjust the statistics to match their vertical

### A/B Testing Recommendations:
- **Subject lines:** Test fear-based ("invisible") vs. opportunity-based ("new channel")
- **CTAs:** Test soft asks ("Worth a look?") vs. specific asks ("Want me to run it?")
- **Length:** Test shorter versions (60 words) vs. full versions (100+ words)

### Follow-up Sequence:
1. **Day 3:** Short follow-up with social proof ("Just helped [Similar Company]...")
2. **Day 7:** Value-add follow-up (share relevant industry report)
3. **Day 14:** Breakup email ("Seems like this isn't a priority right now...")

### Spam Trigger Avoidance:
- ✅ No "free" in subject lines (use "complimentary" instead)
- ✅ No "guaranteed" anywhere
- ✅ No ALL CAPS
- ✅ No excessive punctuation!!!
- ✅ Limited emojis (none in these templates)
- ✅ Conversational tone, not salesy
