# 12 High-Volume, Low-Competition GEO Content Topics for Topify.ai

## Executive Summary

This research identifies 12 high-intent, low-competition content topics where Topify.ai can establish thought leadership in the emerging GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) space. Each topic includes target prompts, current SERP winners, gap analysis, and opportunity scoring.

**Methodology:**
- Analyzed AI search patterns across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini
- Evaluated keyword volume vs. content saturation
- Identified underserved subtopics with commercial intent
- Scored opportunities by: Search Volume × Intent Strength ÷ Competition Level
