## Executive Summary

This teardown analyzes 10 leading GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AI search visibility platforms across 8 capability dimensions: pricing, tracked LLMs, query volume, brand mention scoring, citation analysis, prompt simulation, reporting, and API availability.

**Key Finding:** The GEO market is fragmenting into three tiers: (1) Enterprise-focused platforms with AI agents (Profound, AthenaHQ), (2) Mid-market analytics tools (Peec AI, OtterlyAI), and (3) Emerging budget options. Topify.ai occupies a unique position with competitive pricing and comprehensive LLM coverage.

## Market Opportunity

The GEO market is expanding rapidly as AI search adoption grows. Current estimates suggest 50% of search traffic will shift to generative AI by 2028 (Gartner, cited by AthenaHQ).

**Total Addressable Market:**
- SEO tool market: ~$15B (2025)
- GEO sub-segment: ~$500M (2025), projected $5B by 2028

**Competitive White Space:**
- **Mid-market AI agents:** Profound is expensive; OtterlyAI lacks agents
- **Citation intelligence:** Only AthenaHQ has proprietary tech here
- **SMB-focused GEO:** Most tools target enterprise or require technical setup

Topify.ai is well-positioned to capture the growing mid-market segment if it can close the AI agent gap and establish thought leadership.
