# Mobile Legends Roamer Hero Guide: Top OP Supports in the Current Meta

## Introduction to the Roamer Role

In **Mobile Legends: Bang Bang**, the Roamer (formerly Support) is one of the most impactful roles that can make or break a match. A skilled Roamer controls the early game, secures objectives, and protects carries while creating opportunities for kills.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Vision control and map awareness
- Protecting the marksman/mage carry
- Initiating team fights
- Roaming to assist all lanes
- Securing objectives (Turtle, Lord, Buffs)

---

## S-Tier Roamers (Meta Dominators)

### 1. **Mathilda** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**The Queen of Mobility**

**Why She's OP:**
- Ultimate provides team-wide mobility and engage
- Excellent peel with her knock-up
- Can initiate from anywhere on the map
- Strong early game pressure

**Skill Combo:**
1. Use **Ancestral Guidance** (Passive) to position
2. **Soul Sprint** (Skill 1) for mobility
3. **Guiding Wind** (Skill 2) for shield and damage
4. **Circling Eagle** (Ultimate) for team engage/escape

**Best Build:**
- Courage Mask → Warrior Boots → Athena's Shield
- Immortality → Antique Cuirass → Dominance Ice

**Tips:**
- Coordinate ultimate with jungler for ganks
- Save ultimate for team fights, not solo plays
- Use passive to rotate between lanes quickly

---

### 2. **Chou** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**The Versatile Fighter-Roamer**

**Why He's OP:**
- High damage output for a Roamer
- Excellent crowd control
- Can flex between Roam and Exp Lane
- Strong 1v1 potential

**Skill Combo:**
1. **Jeet Kune Do** (Skill 1) ×2 for mobility
2. **Shunpo** (Skill 2) for positioning
3. **The Way of Dragon** (Ultimate) for knock-up chain

**Best Build:**
- Courage Mask → Warrior Boots → Bloodlust Axe
- Immortality → Athena's Shield → Blade of Despair

**Tips:**
- Master the "Shunpo + Flicker + Ultimate" combo
- Focus on shutting down enemy carries
- Build damage if your team lacks DPS

---

### 3. **Franco** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**The Hook Master**

**Why He's OP:**
- Game-changing hooks can delete enemies
- Ultimate provides suppression (uncleansable)
- Strong crowd control chain
- Excellent pick potential

**Skill Combo:**
1. **Iron Hook** (Skill 1) to catch enemies
2. **Fury Shock** (Skill 2) for slow
3. **Bloody Hunt** (Ultimate) for suppression

**Best Build:**
- Courage Mask → Tough Boots → Athena's Shield
- Immortality → Antique Cuirass → Queen's Wings

**Tips:**
- Practice hook accuracy in custom mode
- Hide in bushes for surprise hooks
- Don't hook tanks unless securing a kill

### 6. **Angela** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**The Best Protector**

## Roamer Gameplay Tips

### Early Game (0-4 minutes)
- **Help jungler secure buffs**
- **Gank mid lane at level 2**
- **Place vision in river bushes**
- **Don't steal minion gold from carries**

### Mid Game (4-12 minutes)
- **Rotate with jungler for ganks**
- **Secure Turtle objectives**
- **Protect your marksman**
- **Clear vision and establish control**

### Late Game (12+ minutes)
- **Stick with your team**
- **Initiate fights or peel for carries**
- **Secure Lord buffs**
- **Control team fight positioning**

## Matchup Guide

### Against Assassins (Ling, Lancelot, Helcurt)
**Pick:** Angela, Mathilda, Chou
**Strategy:** Stay close to carry, save CC for assassin engages

### Against Marksmen (Claude, Karrie, Wanwan)
**Pick:** Franco, Atlas, Tigreal
**Strategy:** Focus on hooks and displacement to delete their damage source

### Against Mages (Lunox, Yve, Xavier)
**Pick:** Lolita, Mathilda
**Strategy:** Block projectiles or use mobility to dodge skillshots

### Against Tanks (Grock, Khufra, Barats)
**Pick:** Chou, Jawhead
**Strategy:** Ignore tanks, focus on deleting their backline

## Item Build Priorities

### Core Roamer Items:
1. **Courage Mask** - Must-have for gold sharing and team buffs
2. **Warrior/Tough Boots** - Based on enemy damage type
3. **Athena's Shield** - Against magic damage
4. **Immortality** - Second life for initiations
5. **Antique Cuirass** - Against physical damage
6. **Dominance Ice** - Anti-heal and slow

### Situational Items:
- **Queen's Wings** - Damage reduction when low HP
- **Radiant Armor** - Against sustained magic damage
- **Blade Armor** - Against basic attack heroes
- **Cursed Helmet** - Magic damage burn
