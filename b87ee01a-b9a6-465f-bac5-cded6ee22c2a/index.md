## Executive Summary

This research identifies 12 strategic content topics that combine high search intent with low competitive density in the AI search visibility space. Topics are ranked by an Opportunity Score (Reach × Inverse Difficulty × Content Gap Severity).

**Key Findings:**
- **Highest Opportunity:** Original benchmark research (#7, Score: 9.7)
- **Fastest Win:** Perplexity-specific optimization (#2, Score: 9.5)

| **Search Volume** | Medium |
| **Competition** | Very Low |
